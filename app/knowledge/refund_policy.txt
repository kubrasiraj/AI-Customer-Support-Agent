Refund Policy

Customers may request a refund within 30 days of receiving an order.

A refund request is eligible when:
1. The product arrived damaged.
2. The wrong product was delivered.
3. The product has a manufacturing defect.

Refunds are not normally available for:
1. Change of mind after the 30-day period.
2. Products damaged by customer misuse.
3. Products without a valid order record.

Refund requests above 10,000 PKR require human approval.

Approved refunds are processed within 5 to 7 business days.

Customers should provide their order number and a clear reason when requesting a refund.
